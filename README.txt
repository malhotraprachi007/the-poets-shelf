THE POET'S SHELF PUBLICATION — WEBSITE PACKAGE

START HERE
- Open index.html in a browser to preview the website.
- Keep the assets folder and all HTML/CSS files together.
- For Netlify Drop, upload the complete the-poets-shelf-site folder (or a ZIP containing index.html at its root).

PAGES
- index.html       Homepage, About, Founder’s Note, Contact and social links
- anthologies.html Anthology catalogue
- team.html        Team members, interns and volunteers
- contests.html    Contest listings
- reviews.html     Testimonials and local-browser review demo

NOTES
- Uploaded images used: logo, founder portrait, Purvi portrait, Ankita portrait, Unbound cover, Instagram and LinkedIn icons.
- The two unavailable anthology covers are represented by designed text covers.
- Certificate PDFs were not uploaded, so certificate links are intentionally omitted.
- Reviews submitted on reviews.html are saved only in the visitor's own browser (localStorage). They are not transmitted to the site owner. A real public review system needs a backend or hosted form/database.
- Contact email opens the visitor's email app using a mailto link.
- Google Fonts are loaded online; system serif/sans-serif fallbacks are included.
