The story behind the shelf

About Us

A creative collective built around the belief that words connect us.

Our Bio

The Poet’s Shelf Publication is a creative collective dedicated to celebrating voices from every corner of the world. We curate anthologies that bring together diverse writers, poets, and storytellers, offering them a platform to share their imagination and experiences. Our mission is to preserve the beauty of words, nurture emerging talent, and create books that resonate with readers across cultures.

How It Started

The journey began with a simple idea: to give writers—especially those just starting out—a space where their words could be seen, valued, and remembered. What started as a passion project grew into a team effort, with volunteers, interns, and contributors joining hands to shape anthologies that reflect creativity without boundaries. From the first manuscript to the latest anthology, The Poet’s Shelf has been built on collaboration, dedication, and the belief that literature can connect hearts and minds.

A note from our founder
“The Poet’s Shelf began from my love of poetry. I started out writing poems myself, and along the way, I found joy in contributing to other publications’ anthologies. But as I watched these contests and collections come to life, I realized we could create something of our own—a space where new voices could be heard and where creativity truly thrives.

That’s how The Poet’s Shelf came to be. We began hosting our own anthology contests, building projects that turned words into memorable collections. Along the way, we grew as a team, welcoming others who shared our vision. And here we are today—creating anthologies that celebrate the beauty of storytelling, from first drafts to published pages.”
— Prachi Malhotra, Founder
